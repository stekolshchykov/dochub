For editors and testers

Translation a shortcut away.

KeyAct puts Apple-powered translation, word tools and local voice notes in the Mac menu bar, with explicit language choices and keyboard access.

Product facts

  • Native macOS app, macOS 15 or later. The App Store candidate is version 1.0.0, build 6; publication is not yet approved.
  • One-time purchase, no subscription. Planned U.S. price: $5; local storefront prices use Apple's regional pricing.
  • Apple translation, related words, parts of speech, spelling, dictionary lookup, voice notes, history and custom actions.
  • A finite bundled offline conjugation catalog contains verb forms for 14 languages. Coverage varies by language and verb.
  • Apple resources have different language coverage; some require downloading. Voice recognition requires a supported on-device route for the selected locale.
  • The interface is in English; product captions and help are available in English and Russian.

Useful review angles

Stay with the writing task

Select languages, translate, and use the result without moving to a browser dashboard.

Explore one word

Compare related words, inspect word classes and copy or speak an individual result.

Language availability is explicit

The resource assistant distinguishes installed, available and unsupported capabilities instead of silently changing the language.

A voice note on your terms

Choose the recognition locale, audio location and whether to save audio, transcription or both.

Testing and boundaries

The sandbox App Store edition uses manual input or the clipboard, URL/HTTP actions and user-installed executable scripts. Typed shell commands and Accessibility-based selection belong to the separate full local edition. Do not describe the App Store edition as an unrestricted terminal.

No developer-operated analytics SDK, account or advertising. Optional online lookup contacts a third-party dictionary service; see the privacy policy. Offline language support is not universal.

For testing access, corrections or press questions: admin@raindigit.ie. Include the platform and version you want to test; never send private text, credentials or recordings.
