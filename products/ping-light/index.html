Export safely →

Local monitoring, not a 24/7 cloud service. Checks require Ping Light to be running and your Mac to be awake and connected. Sleep, a closed app, loss of internet or denied network permissions can interrupt checks.

For continuous monitoring while your Mac is off, use a separate always-on service. Ping Light is for a quick, local view of the endpoints you can reach from this Mac.
