Planned access

The planned launch model is a free download with a one-time purchase to unlock Results. The target US price is about $8; the App Store will show the final price for each region. Purchasing and restoring purchases are not available in the current development version.

Understand the limits

A comparison reflects your inputs and priorities. A score is not a probability of success, professional advice or a guaranteed outcome. Results can show a tie, unmet conditions or a need for more information.
