Free download and one-time Results unlock

The app is free to download. Create and edit projects, criteria and options for free, and explore the learning example including its Results. A one-time purchase unlocks Results for your own projects. This is not a subscription. The app shows the localized price supplied by Apple before purchase. Restore Purchases is available from the Results purchase panel.

Understand the limits

A comparison reflects your inputs and priorities. A score is not a probability of success, professional advice or a guaranteed outcome. Results can show a tie, unmet conditions or a need for more information.
